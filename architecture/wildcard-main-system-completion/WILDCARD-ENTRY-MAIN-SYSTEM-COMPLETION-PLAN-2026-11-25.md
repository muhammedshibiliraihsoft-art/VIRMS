## 2. Canonical contracts to retain

- Read only relevant sections of `README.md` and `docs-final-v2/02-user-roles-permissions.md`, `03-system-modules.md`, `05-vehicle-investor-finance-workflow.md`, `07-ui-navigation-structure.md`, `09-company-accounting-owner-distribution.md`, and `10-garage-jobs-workflow.md`; inspect source for implemented behavior. Do not use `/docs-final/`, `/docs-legacy/` or older V1 proposals as governing product rules.
- One Company/Tenant owns Branches. Vehicle Management owns one stable Vehicle ID and movement history. `STOCK`/`EXTERNAL_CUSTOMER` belongs to each Garage **Job**, not the Vehicle. The same Vehicle can later have an external-customer Job after Investor responsibility ends. Job contact Name/Phone is service context, not ownership or a unique customer key; mandatory input meaning remains unresolved in V2. Garage operates with Investment OFF for customer work. Enforce authentication, same Company, effective Branch, module ON, action permission and object scope in backend, including mutation and list/detail queries.
- Garage owns work, approved Job cost, customer charge and outstanding. Accounting owns actual Cash/Bank movement and one typed receipt/revenue source. Link Job to Accounting event ID. Never post a second cash event or derive receipt from charge. External receipt reference is unique Company-wide; enforce charge cap atomically under concurrent receipts. Corrections retain history.
- Company result is Vehicle Company Share + Garage Revenue + Other Company Income − Company Expenses, counted once per source. Running Capital is capital, not revenue/expense or Investor principal. Branch filter changes attribution view; Company result consolidates Branches. Company period confirmation, loss carryforward, protected Owner split snapshot and actual partial payments remain distinct records.
- UI role hiding is presentation only. No browser-side authorization or cross-tenant trust. Use INR fixed-precision backend values, not JS floating-point financial recomputation for payable values. A failed/unavailable source shows unavailable, not a synthetic zero. Module OFF must preserve authorized history while denying unapproved new actions.
