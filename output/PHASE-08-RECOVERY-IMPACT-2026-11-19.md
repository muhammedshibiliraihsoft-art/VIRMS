## Current source and desired outcome

V2 05 sections 2–4 require actual Buyer receipt, Investor principal and Company Running Capital recovery to be distinct; a second cash movement must not be created for recovery. A same-Company Company-level Accountant confirms recovery (Phase 8 architecture, confirmed follow-up). Full collection, recovery and resolved refund obligations gate ordinary Investor Profit payment. `apps/investment/services.py:create_vehicle_profit_payment` currently blocks all new payments with `PRINCIPAL_RECOVERY_REQUIRED`. Existing idempotent replays and prior payment history must survive.

`apps/vehicles/services.py:create_sale_receipt` locks the Sale and creates one receipt-linked Accounting event and movement. Sale corrections and refunds are separate immutable records and retain actual cash history. Investor Fund currently uses `InvestorFundCredit` lots and use/return allocations: Investment and Vehicle `_available_credit`, Investment `fund_summary`, Vehicle funding and corrections, and `portal._investor_portal_snapshot` derive balances independently. Accounting migration 0005 requires every Fund credit to link to its own equal Capital Inflow event and movement. A naive additional credit therefore double-counts Buyer cash. Investment migration 0006 ties Fund-use adjustments to cost corrections and enforces the source allocation total; simply releasing a use allocation for recovery would violate that invariant.

## Proposed engineering direction — not yet implemented

Introduce append-only, Company-scoped recovery classifications linked by foreign keys to the Sale and actual receipt sources, with separate Investor and Company amounts, author, module revisions, source version and audited idempotent confirmation. Keep the existing Buyer receipt as the sole actual Cash/Bank inflow. A separate traceable recovery-lot mechanism or a reviewed extension of the existing credit-lot contract must support subsequent Fund use and return without a second Accounting movement. Select the exact storage and database guards only after tracing all lot-allocation and correction constraints; historical credits retain their current Capital Inflow requirement. Running Capital recovery remains a separate capital classification, not Company Income or Profit.

Investor-only partial actual receipts may support principal-first classification within actual collected and outstanding principal caps. Mixed-funded positive settlement must honor V2's full-collection and separate capital reconciliation rules. Derived positions must distinguish confirmed recovery, current applied amounts, refund reserves, uncovered refund payable, Fund available and historically recovered principal. Calculation or preview must never post a recovery.

## Unresolved business meaning

V2 05 section 2 says a mixed-funded Vehicle's Investor principal recovery, Company Running Capital recovery and positive result must reconcile to a corrected Sale and actual refund. Example: Investor capital 60, Company capital 40, collected Sale 120, then corrected Sale 80 and refund payable 40. **User decision, 2026-11-19:** the Company Accountant reviews each case and decides how the resulting Loss affects the Investor and Company. This is consistent with the existing V2 manual Loss confirmation, explicit amounts, reason and audit rules; do not infer an automatic loss waterfall from principal-first positive settlement. This decision does not by itself specify how the 40 refund cash payment is split among accounting components or how unavailable Fund is reserved; trace the existing V2 rules and ask only if a remaining concrete ambiguity cannot be resolved from those rules. Implementation and validation remain outstanding.

The effective agreement snapshot and historical Company Share correction path for this case require source/decision reconciliation before enabling settlement.
